# Celebrity list

`top5500.csv` lists 5,500 famous people for celebrity chart pages.

- **Source:** Pantheon 2025 person dataset by Datawheel (https://pantheon.world), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. Credit "Pantheon by Datawheel" wherever this data is shown.
- **Groups:** `Modern` (born 1960 or later, ranked by page views), `All-time` (born 1850 to 1959, ranked by fame score), `Historical` (born before 1850).
- **Birth times:** filled only where an original source was found (birth record, birth notice, the person's own words, official announcement or biography), with `time_source_link`, `rating` (Rodden scale) and a quote in `notes`. The top 300 were researched on 5 Nov 2026.
- **Jesus:** proposed chart supplied by the owner, rated XX (date in question).

Apart from the Jesus proposed chart, which the owner supplied, no birth times here come from Astro-Databank, Astrotheme, Astro-Seek or other chart sites.
