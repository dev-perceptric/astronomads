# Content calendar: December 2026 to December 2027

What Astronomads publishes, when, and what it costs. Astrotheme's menu was used as a map of what a full astrology site covers. Every word we publish is our own, and every sky date below was computed with our own engine (`astronomy-engine`, true zodiac of date, dates in UTC) on 6 November 2026. Recheck a date with the engine before its post goes out.

We skip Astrotheme's Sabian-style degree symbols (the classic set is still under copyright) and write our own 360 degree meanings instead.

## 5. Celebrities

- `data/celebs/top5500.csv` has birth dates and cities for 5,500 people (Pantheon 2025, CC BY-SA 4.0, credit "Pantheon by Datawheel").
- **Now:** date-only charts. Planets and signs, no houses or Ascendant, marked "birth time unknown". Ten a week, most famous first, each with a short reading in our words. Also "Famous people born under Leo" lists for the 12 signs.
- **Timed charts:** only with a sourced time and its Rodden rating (11 so far). Never from Astrotheme, Astro-Databank, Astro-Seek or other chart sites without a license. With an Astro-Databank license (USD 979.30 for 5 years, see `docs/plan.md`), about 40% of the list would get rated times.
- **Feature posts:** famous couples' compatibility (date-only), birthdays of the week.

## 6. Rules for every piece

- Our own words, written fresh. Astrotheme and other sites are a map of topics only, never a source of text.
- Every sky date comes from our engine and is checked again the week it goes out.
- Plain words, the brand voice in `docs/brand.md`, and the copy rules in `interpretations.test.ts`.
- One page per question people search; each links to the tool that answers it.
- No fortune telling, and no health, money or legal promises.
